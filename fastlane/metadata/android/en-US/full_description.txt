Gompashi is a minimalist compass that does not point north — it points to the nearest
state liquor store in Finland (Alko), Sweden (Systembolaget) or Norway (Vinmonopolet).

A bottle-shaped needle rotates toward the closest store and shows the distance, the
store name and an opening-hours countdown. A toggle switches between the nearest and
second-nearest store.

Features:
- Works offline: the store register travels with the app.
- Walking-route map (OpenStreetMap) with an estimated step count.
- Download map regions for fully offline use, including on-device route calculation.
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Uses the device's own location only.

Store data: Alko, Systembolaget and OpenStreetMap (Vinmonopolet). Map tiles by CARTO
(OpenStreetMap data). Routing by the OSM/OSRM service when online, on-device otherwise.
